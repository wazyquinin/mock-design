What's broken today

  1. Layers are cut off. The tree layer (E4) is cropped flat at the top of its band, so pine tips end in a straight horizontal line.
  2. Five art styles at once. A flat digital sky, pasted cloud sprites, painted ridges, a photo-real eagle, fox and balloon, plus a CSS blur for the sun. It reads as a collage.
  3. Characters float. A goat stands in mid-sky, the owl hangs off the screen edge, and eagles fly across the logo.
  4. Clock collides with the scene, and night inputs fail contrast.
  5. Time of day barely shows, and there's no single entrance.

How this version is made

  • A real photograph of an alpine valley is the land. It's one image, so there are no seams, no cut-out fringes and no mismatched styles.
  • Depth from AI. A depth map (Depth Anything V2, run offline) gives every pixel a distance. The shader uses it for true parallax as the pointer moves, haze that grows with distance, and wind that only moves the near pines.
  • A live sky. The photo's sky is masked out (the ridge line is hand-traced and edge-snapped) and replaced by a procedural one. Clouds drift and are lit from the sun's side. The sun sets behind the real peaks, and the moon shows its real phase. Night adds stars, the Milky Way and an aurora.
  • Real light changes. The photo is re-graded through the day: blue-hour moonlight at night, pink dawn, neutral noon, warm golden hour with alpenglow on the snow. Cloud shadows sweep across the valley, and mist pools in it at dawn and night.
  • Birds are drawn as real silhouettes: soaring raptors with fingered wingtips, and a fast flock of swifts.
  • Smooth by design. It's one GPU pass per frame over a static photo; the sky detail is only computed where the sky is visible.
  • Reduced motion freezes clouds, wind and birds, and skips the entrance.

How it would port

  • Replace PaintedEnvironment and the cast with one ValleyScene component (a WebGL canvas, the photo and a data texture). Keep the static photo as the reduced-motion / no-WebGL fallback.
  • Fold celestial.ts into the palette clock (the arc and lunar-phase maths carry over).
  • Keep the existing contracts: playExit() with its 900 ms safety race, the null→error shake, and skipViewTransition.
