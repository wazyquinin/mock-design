Security & deployment

Every sensitive change leaves a trail no one can quietly edit.

Changes are written to a hash-chained audit log. Each entry carries the fingerprint of the one before it, so tampering with history breaks the chain.

Tenant isolation

Each organisation’s records are scoped to its own tenant, in the app and at the database layer.

Signed licences

Licence files are cryptographically signed and verified by the deployment itself.

Your identity provider

SAML/OIDC single sign-on with SCIM provisioning on Enterprise.

Logs you can review

Structured logs, metrics and the audit trail, available for your own review.

    FAQ

    Questions before you start.

    Something else? Talk to us.

    Can I change plans later?

    Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are billed pro rata. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

    Is there a free trial?

    The Free plan has no time limit and needs no credit card. Paid plans include a 14-day free trial.

    What payment methods do you accept?

    Major credit and debit cards, UPI, net banking, and bank transfer for annual plans.

    What happens to my data if I cancel?

    Your data stays available for 30 days so you can export it as CSV. After that it is securely deleted.

    Can we run it on our own servers?

    Yes, on the Enterprise plan. The licence is signed and verified by the install itself, so it can run offline.
